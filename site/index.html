nostr/relay-bench

Nostr relay bench

Every actively developed Nostr relay, built from its latest release, loaded with the same million events on the same machine every week, checked against the protocol, and scored on what operators and clients need. Pick yours above to see where it stands and what would move it most.

Performance is each relay against strfry in the same run (strfry = 100) across writing, reading, live delivery and connections. Correctness is the share of relay-tester's required checks passed by the relay as shipped. Capability is the weighted score out of 100. Click a column to sort and a row for the details.

Choose a relay to see its ranks, where it sits in the field, and a ranked list of what would gain it the most. The choice is kept in the link.

Each panel is the geometric mean of that area's tests, strfry = 100, on a log scale. The coloured areas make up the performance index; the grey ones are reported but left out of it. A missing bar says why: not supported, refused by the relay's policy, or wrong answers (a fast wrong answer isn't counted). The Tests tab has every number behind these.

Every test in the run, in plain words: what it asks the relay, which client behaviour it stands for, and every relay's raw result. Pick a test on the left.

Each relay scores 0 to 3 on each criterion (none, partial, good, best in class), times its weight. Hover a cell for the evidence behind it; click a relay's name to compare as it. Scores are judgement calls from code and docs; disagree in a PR to capability/relays/.

0 none or undocumented 1 partial 2 good 3 best in class

Active, slowing and dormant come from Vitor's commit survey of 19 Nov 2026; "Missed" were not in it. Commit counts are refreshed weekly. To add one, send a PR to catalog.toml.

Harness, configs and raw results: github.com/0ceanSlim/nostr-relay-bench. If your relay's config is unfair to it, send a PR.
